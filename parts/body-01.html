Adaptive UK Client Fact Find & Financial Planning Profile

A rule-based adaptive pre-advice fact find that changes the interview around the person, couple or family while organising the information a UK financial planner, accountant or other professional would normally need. It can be completed anonymously as Client A / Client B.

Privacy: this file does not send your answers anywhere. Answers are stored only in this browser using local storage until you clear them. Do not enter names, addresses, account numbers, passwords, National Insurance numbers, creditor references or security information.
Important: this is a pre-advice information pack, not an FCA-prescribed form and not regulated financial, tax or legal advice. A professional firm may still need its own identity, anti-money-laundering, consent and suitability paperwork before giving advice.

Before you begin

You do not need financial knowledge to complete this. The aim is to create a professional-quality picture of the household before anybody recommends a product or charges for work that could have been prepared in advance.

Leave unknowns unknownIf you do not know a figure or answer, leave it blank or choose “Unsure”. A missing fact is more useful than a guessed one.
Estimates are acceptableFor household spending, asset values and similar figures, a sensible estimate is useful. You can mark how reliable the figures are.
No identity neededDo not enter names, addresses, account numbers, passwords, National Insurance numbers, creditor references or security codes.
What happens to your answers?
  • This page contains no analytics, adverts, trackers or external scripts.
  • Nothing is automatically uploaded or emailed.
  • Answers are saved only in this browser so you can stop and continue later on the same device.
  • At the end, you choose whether to copy or download an anonymous scenario.
  • On a shared device, clear the saved answers when finished.
This interview adapts as you answer. Irrelevant sections are skipped. Relevant answers can open deeper questions—for example children, mortgages, an IVA, self-employment, investments or a second adult’s finances. The branching is rule-based in your browser; no AI service receives the answers while you complete it.
Not advice: the questionnaire organises facts, evidence and unresolved questions. It deliberately separates things you can check yourself from matters that may warrant an accountant, solicitor, insolvency practitioner or FCA-authorised adviser.
